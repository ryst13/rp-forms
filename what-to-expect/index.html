Your project, step by step

Six steps, in the order they happen.

  1. Day one

    Approve and book

    Approve your estimate in the client portal. Your contract and a 30% deposit invoice follow by email. Signed and paid means you're booked.

  2. Right after

    Colors and access

    Fill in the Color & Access Form in your portal: colors surface by surface, how we get in, parking and timing. Do it even if we're matching what's there. Early finishers get first pick of start dates.

  3. When your slot comes up

    Your start date

    Projects are scheduled in booking order. We email you a proposed date; pick it or tell us what works better. Before you start, we confirm the day, the planned finish and what to have ready.

  4. On site

    During the job

    The crew arrives between 8 and 9am and cleans up at the end of every day. You don't need to be home. You'll hear from us whenever something changes.

  5. If needed

    Changes along the way

    Find something the estimate didn't cover, like rot behind a board? We price it in writing as an Additional Work Order. Nothing extra happens until you approve it.

  6. The end

    Finishing up

    We walk the job first and fix anything that isn't right, then walk it with you. Once you're happy, we send the final invoice and leave labeled touch-up paint.

Getting your home ready

The same list we send two days before your start.

Interior
  • Move small items, pictures and breakables out of the rooms being painted.
  • Clear the tops of dressers, counters and shelves.
  • Pull furniture about three feet from the walls if you can. We cover and move the rest.
  • Take down any curtains or blinds you want kept clear of paint.
  • Plan for pets to be somewhere calm.
  • Tell us the day before if anything about access has changed.
Exterior
  • Move cars off the driveway and away from the house.
  • Move grills, planters, furniture and hoses away from the walls.
  • Close and lock the windows near the work.
  • Trim back shrubs touching the house if you can.
  • Check the outdoor spigot and outlet are on and working.
  • Give your neighbors a heads-up if we'll be working near the property line.

Good to know

Answers to the questions clients ask us most.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most clients leave us a lockbox or a key. Tell us what works on your Color & Access Form.

Will it smell?

Very little. Our paint is low-odor, and bedrooms get zero-VOC paint, so you can sleep in the room the same night.

I live in a condo

We send a certificate of insurance to your building manager or condo trust, and we work within building rules.

What if I'm not happy?

Tell us at the walkthrough or any time after. We're local and only a phone call away, and we come back to make it right.

Weather and exterior work

Exterior paint needs dry surfaces and the right temperature. Rain, high wind and cold stop the work. If we move your dates for weather, rebooking costs you nothing.

35°F lowest we paint45°F and rising preferred

Homes built before 1978

Most Boston homes have lead paint somewhere under the surface. We're EPA RRP and Massachusetts lead-safe certified, and we set up containment before we disturb old paint. Keep children and pets out of the work area until we've cleaned up.
